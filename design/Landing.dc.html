The 31 March rule

Stop asking. Start reminding them what they lose.

Buyers who don't pay a micro or small supplier by 31 March can't claim that purchase as an expense that year. Late amounts also carry interest at three times the RBI bank rate. Dues Banshee turns those rules into a countdown on every reminder.
