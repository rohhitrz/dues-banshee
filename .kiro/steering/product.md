# Product: Dues Banshee

An AI collections agent for Indian MSME suppliers. It chases unpaid invoices from the supplier's own buyers over WhatsApp and voice (Hindi/English), and uses Indian payment law (MSMED Act; Income-tax s.43B(h) / s.37(2)(g)) as leverage.

## Users
- Small manufacturer, job-work unit or service provider registered on Udyam as micro/small: Hindi-first, WhatsApp-native. NOT wholesale/retail traders (excluded from MSMED delayed-payment rules).

## Core principles
1. **Lead with the buyer's self-interest:** the 31 March tax-bomb, statutory interest. Never beg, never threaten.
2. **A human approves outbound escalation:** calls, legal notices and Samadhaan packs need explicit owner approval.
3. **Every legal claim cites a rule ID** from `legal-rules.md`. If no rule applies, don't make the claim.
4. **Protect the relationship:** tone is firm and respectful ("Sharma ji"), and pauses when the buyer promises to pay.
5. **Not legal advice.** Every outbound document carries the disclaimer.

## Theme
Halloween/Kiroween. Unpaid invoices are tombstones in a graveyard. Payment "resurrects" them. The theme must always encode real data (height = amount, moss = days overdue, glow = promise-to-pay), never pure decoration.

## Out of scope
Real calls to Indian phone numbers, live Tally integration, automatic Samadhaan filing, multi-tenant billing.
